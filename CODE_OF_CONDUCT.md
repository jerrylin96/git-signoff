# Code of conduct

This project asks people to state, in their own words, what they understand
and what they are accountable for. The same standard applies to how we treat
each other here.

## Expectations

- Be direct about the work and respectful of the person. Critique code,
  specs, and claims; do not attack contributors.
- Assume competence and good faith. Ask before concluding.
- Keep disagreements on the evidence: a failing test, a conformance vector, a
  reproducible run, a cited document.
- Respect people's time: read the relevant docs before asking, and answer
  questions you are asked.
- Harassment, discrimination, demeaning language, sustained disruption, and
  publishing others' private information are not acceptable anywhere the
  project is discussed (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, email).

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces and to anyone representing the project
elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer through GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting form on this repository (it reaches only the maintainer) or by a
direct message on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. Responses range
from a private word to removal from project spaces, at the maintainer's
judgment and proportionate to the behavior.
